About AI Response Cleaner

AI Response Cleaner is a free browser-based tool for inspecting and cleaning text generated by modern AI assistants. It is useful when an AI response contains hidden Unicode characters such as zero-width spaces, zero-width joiners, directional formatting characters, invisible separators, control characters, and other Unicode characters that may not be visible when reading the text.

The tool runs locally in your browser. Your pasted text is processed by the page itself and does not need to be uploaded to a server. This makes it useful for cleaning AI-generated text before copying, publishing, storing, analyzing, or processing it elsewhere.
